  • 1.1
    FILM
    Tacita Dean, 2011
    A tribute to analogue film; the most familiar position in cinema: simply watching.
  • 1.2
    Looking Back: Fourth-Wall Clips
    Various directors, 1960–2019
    Seven characters turn to the camera and look straight at us.
  • 1.3
    Performer/Audience/Mirror
    Dan Graham, 1975
    The audience watches itself being watched and described.
  • 2.1
    Forest of Us
    Es Devlin, 2021
    A film opens into a mirror maze, inspired by The Maze Runner.
  • 3.1
    Line Describing a Cone
    Anthony McCall, 1973
    Projected light becomes a cone that visitors can stand inside.
  • 3.2
    Floor Film
    Tony Hill, 1975
    Visitors stand on the image itself.
  • 3.3
    The Inverted Room
    Curatorial design, after Inception
    A mirrored ceiling shows visitors upside down inside the picture.
  • 4.1
    In the Scene
    Curatorial installation, after The Truman Show
    The screen turns into a mirror; visitors appear in the film's sky set.
